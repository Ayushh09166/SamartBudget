Calculation Methodology

SmartSave evaluates timeline projections using straightforward surplus and target differentials.

Step 1 · Monthly Surplus
Monthly Saving = Income − Expenses

Subtract routine living expenses from total monthly income to determine net disposable surplus.

Step 2 · Target Gap
Remaining Target = Target − Current Savings

Deduct liquid funds already secured to pinpoint the exact capital required.

Step 3 · Projected Timeline
Estimated Months = Remaining ÷ Monthly Saving

Divide the remaining target by your monthly surplus to calculate the timeline to completion.
